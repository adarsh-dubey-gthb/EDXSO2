INDIAN INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (IIT) DELHI - ACADEMIC FINANCIAL AID.
        MERIT-CUM-MEANS (MCM) SCHOLARSHIP.
        Eligibility Criteria: Undergraduate B.Tech and Dual Degree students whose gross family annual income does not exceed ₹4.5 Lakh and CGPA is minimum 6.0.
        Academic Requirements: Minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 6.0 with no backlogs.
        Financial Benefit Amount: 100% Tuition fee waiver plus ₹1,000 per month pocket allowance.
        Family Income Limit: Gross family annual income not exceeding ₹4.5 Lakh per annum.
        Application Deadline: 15 September 2026.
        Official Portal Source: https://home.iitd.ac.in.
        Direct Application Portal: https://eacademic.iitd.ac.in.
        Selection Process: Strict verification of income documentation and CGPA ranking up to 25% of batch size.
        Documents Required: Parental income certificate / ITR V acknowledgement, Form 16, Salary slip, Affidavit on stamp paper.
