INDIAN INSTITUTE OF SCIENCE (IISC) BANGALORE.
        FINANCIAL SUPPORT AND RESEARCH FELLOWSHIPS.
        Eligibility Criteria: Regular full-time PhD and M.Tech (Research) students admitted through national level tests (GATE / CSIR-UGC NET / JEST).
        Academic Requirements: Bachelor's / Master's degree in Engineering / Science with valid national test score.
        Course Level: Ph.D. / M.Tech. (Research).
        Financial Benefit Amount: ₹37,000 per month for first 2 years; ₹42,000 per month for subsequent 3 years plus contingency grant.
        Family Income Limit: Not specified.
        Application Deadline: 31 March 2026.
        Official Portal Source: https://iisc.ac.in.
        Direct Application Portal: https://admissions.iisc.ac.in.
        Selection Process: Departmental research interview and technical evaluation.
        Documents Required: GATE/NET scorecard, Degree transcripts, Research interest statement, Caste/EWS certificate if applicable.
