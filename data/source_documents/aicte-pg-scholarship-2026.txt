ALL INDIA COUNCIL FOR TECHNICAL EDUCATION - POST GRADUATE (PG) SCHOLARSHIP.
        Eligibility Criteria: Full-time GATE / CEED qualified students admitted to AICTE approved M.E./M.Tech/M.Pharm/M.Arch/M.Des programs.
        Academic Requirements: Valid GATE or CEED scorecard at the time of admission.
        Course Level: Postgraduate (M.Tech / M.E. / M.Des / M.Pharm).
        Financial Benefit Amount: ₹12,400 per month for 24 months (2 academic years).
        Family Income Limit: Not specified (GATE/CEED score based).
        Application Deadline: 30 November 2026.
        Official Portal Source: https://www.aicte-india.org/schemes/students-development-schemes.
        Direct Application Portal: https://pgscholarship.aicte-india.org.
        Selection Process: Direct benefit transfer to all GATE/CEED qualified students admitted to AICTE approved courses.
        Documents Required: GATE/CEED scorecard, Institute admission slip, Bank account linked with Aadhaar (DBT active).
