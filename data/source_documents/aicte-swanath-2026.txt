ALL INDIA COUNCIL FOR TECHNICAL EDUCATION - SWANATH SCHOLARSHIP SCHEME.
        Eligibility Criteria: Orphan students, or candidates whose both parents died due to Covid-19, or wards of Armed Forces and Central Paramilitary Forces martyred in action.
        Academic Requirements: Currently studying in AICTE approved degree/diploma courses.
        Financial Benefit Amount: ₹50,000 per annum for every year of study.
        Family Income Limit: Family annual income not more than ₹8 Lakh per annum.
        Application Deadline: 31 October 2026.
        Official Portal Source: https://www.aicte-india.org/schemes/students-development-schemes.
        Direct Application Portal: https://scholarships.gov.in.
        Selection Process: Fulfillment of eligibility and verification by institute nodal officer and AICTE.
        Documents Required: Death certificate of parents / Martyr proof, Bonafide student certificate, Income certificate, Bank details.
