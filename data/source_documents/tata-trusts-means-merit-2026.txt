TATA TRUSTS INDIVIDUAL GRANTS PROGRAMME - EDUCATION GRANTS (MEANS AND MERIT).
        Implemented by Tata Trusts.
        Eligibility Criteria: Indian students enrolled in undergraduate or postgraduate courses in accredited institutions in India.
        Academic Requirements: Consistent academic record with at least 65% aggregate in preceding academic exams.
        Financial Benefit Amount: Partial tuition fee reimbursement (₹30,000 to ₹1,00,000).
        Family Income Limit: Family annual income below ₹4.5 Lakh.
        Application Deadline: 15 January 2027.
        Official Portal Source: https://www.tatatrusts.org/our-work/individual-grants-programme/education-grants.
        Direct Application Portal: https://www.tatatrusts.org/our-work/individual-grants-programme/education-grants.
        Selection Process: Assessment of financial hardship, academic consistency, and verification of genuine fee deficit.
        Documents Required: College fee structure receipt, Previous mark sheets, Family salary slips or Form 16/income certificate, Aadhaar card.
