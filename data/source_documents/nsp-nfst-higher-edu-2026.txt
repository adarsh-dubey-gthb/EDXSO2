MINISTRY OF TRIBAL AFFAIRS, GOVERNMENT OF INDIA.
        NATIONAL FELLOWSHIP AND SCHOLARSHIP FOR HIGHER EDUCATION OF ST STUDENTS.
        Eligibility Criteria: ST candidates who have secured admission in premier institutions across India for degree/post-graduate courses.
        Academic Requirements: Admission in top-tier national institutions (IITs, NITs, NIFT, IIMs).
        Financial Benefit Amount: Full tuition fee reimbursement plus living allowance of ₹3,000 per month plus ₹19,000 per year book allowance.
        Family Income Limit: Total family annual income not exceeding ₹6.0 Lakh per annum.
        Category: ST.
        Application Deadline: 31 October 2026.
        Official Portal Source: https://scholarships.gov.in.
        Direct Application Portal: https://scholarships.gov.in.
        Selection Process: Merit-based slot allocation up to 1,000 fresh scholarships every year.
        Documents Required: ST Certificate, Income certificate, College admission allotment letter, Fee voucher, Aadhaar.
