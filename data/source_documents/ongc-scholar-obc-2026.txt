ONGC SCHOLAR PORTAL - SCHOLARSHIP SCHEME FOR OBC STUDENTS.
        Eligibility Criteria: OBC students in 1st year of Engineering, MBBS, MBA, or Master's in Geosciences with minimum 60% marks in qualifying examination.
        Academic Requirements: Minimum 60% marks in Class 12 or graduation.
        Financial Benefit Amount: ₹48,000 per annum.
        Family Income Limit: Family annual income not more than ₹2 Lakh per annum.
        Age Limit: Maximum 30 years.
        Category: OBC (Non-Creamy Layer).
        Application Deadline: 30 November 2026.
        Official Portal Source: https://www.ongcscholar.org.
        Direct Application Portal: https://www.ongcscholar.org/portal/site/ongc-scholar.
        Selection Process: Zone-wise merit list based on qualifying examination percentages.
        Documents Required: OBC NCL Certificate, Income certificate, Marksheets, Bank passbook copy.
