DEPARTMENT OF HIGHER EDUCATION, MINISTRY OF EDUCATION.
        CENTRAL SECTOR SCHEME OF SCHOLARSHIP FOR COLLEGE AND UNIVERSITY STUDENTS (PM-USP).
        Eligibility Criteria: Students above 80th percentile of successful candidates in relevant stream from respective Class 12 board, pursuing regular undergraduate course.
        Academic Requirements: Score above 80th percentile in Class 12 board examinations.
        Financial Benefit Amount: ₹12,000 per annum for undergraduate (1st 3 years); ₹20,000 per annum for post-graduate.
        Family Income Limit: Gross parental annual income not exceeding ₹4.5 Lakh per annum.
        Age Limit: 18 to 25 years.
        Application Deadline: 31 October 2026.
        Official Portal Source: https://scholarships.gov.in.
        Direct Application Portal: https://scholarships.gov.in.
        Selection Process: Board-wise state quota allocation based on population and percentile rank.
        Documents Required: Class 12 marksheet, Income certificate, College bonafide certificate, Aadhaar seeded bank account proof.
