MINISTRY OF EDUCATION, GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - PRIME MINISTER'S RESEARCH FELLOWSHIP (PMRF).
        Eligibility Criteria: Direct entry or lateral entry PhD candidates admitted to IISc, IITs, IISERs, or central universities with minimum 8.0 CGPA or qualifying GATE score.
        Academic Requirements: B.Tech/M.Tech/M.Sc with CGPA >= 8.0 from recognized central institution or GATE score >= 650.
        Course Level: Ph.D. (Doctoral).
        Financial Benefit Amount: ₹70,000 to ₹80,000 per month (₹70,000/month Year 1-2, ₹75,000/month Year 3, ₹80,000/month Year 4-5) plus ₹2,00,000 annual research grant.
        Family Income Limit: Not specified.
        Application Deadline: 15 June 2026.
        Official Portal Source: https://www.pmrf.in.
        Direct Application Portal: https://www.pmrf.in.
        Selection Process: Rigorous national level review of research proposal followed by interview by National Subject Committees.
        Documents Required: Research proposal, Academic transcripts, GATE/qualifying exam score card, Recommendation letters from supervisor.
