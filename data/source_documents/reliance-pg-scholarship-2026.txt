RELIANCE FOUNDATION POSTGRADUATE SCHOLARSHIPS - FUTURE TECHNOLOGIES.
        Eligibility Criteria: First-year full-time postgraduate students in select future-tech areas (Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Mathematics & Computing, Electrical/Electronics, Renewable Energy, Material Sciences).

        Course Level: Postgraduate (M.Tech / M.E. / M.Sc. in STEM).
        Financial Benefit Amount: Up to ₹6,00,000 over the duration of degree.
        Family Income Limit: Not specified (merit and interview based).
        Application Deadline: 20 October 2026.
        Official Portal Source: https://scholarships.reliancefoundation.org.
        Direct Application Portal: https://scholarships.reliancefoundation.org.
        Selection Process: Application screening, subject-matter expert evaluation, and interview by eminent panel of scientists and leaders.
        Documents Required: Undergraduate transcripts, GATE scorecard, Statement of purpose, Two letters of recommendation, Admission proof.
