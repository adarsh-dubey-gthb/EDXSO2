UNIVERSITY GRANTS COMMISSION (UGC) - NATIONAL TESTING AGENCY (NTA).
        JUNIOR RESEARCH FELLOWSHIP (JRF).
        Eligibility Criteria: Candidates who qualify UGC-NET for Junior Research Fellowship and register for M.Phil/Ph.D. in recognized university.
        Academic Requirements: Master's degree with at least 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwD).
        Course Level: Ph.D. / M.Phil.
        Financial Benefit Amount: ₹37,000 per month (JRF for first 2 years); ₹42,000 per month (SRF remaining 3 years) plus HRA and contingency.
        Family Income Limit: Not specified.
        Age Limit: Not more than 30 years (relaxation for reserved categories).
        Application Deadline: 15 October 2026.
        Official Portal Source: https://ugcnet.nta.ac.in.
        Direct Application Portal: https://ugcnet.nta.ac.in.
        Selection Process: National eligibility test conducted twice a year by National Testing Agency (NTA).
        Documents Required: UGC-NET JRF award letter, Master's degree certificate, PhD admission registration letter, Joining report.
