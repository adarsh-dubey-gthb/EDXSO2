DEFENCE RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT ORGANISATION (DRDO) - MINISTRY OF DEFENCE.
        DRDO SCHOLARSHIP SCHEME FOR GIRLS IN AEROSPACE & AERONAUTICAL ENGINEERING (2023 CYCLE).
        Eligibility Criteria: Female Indian students who secured admission to 1st year of B.E./B.Tech/M.E./M.Tech in Aerospace/Aeronautical/Avionics Engineering with valid JEE Main or GATE score.
        Academic Requirements: Valid JEE Main / GATE score in engineering disciplines.
        Financial Benefit Amount: ₹1,55,000 per annum for M.Tech; ₹1,20,000 per annum for B.Tech.
        Family Income Limit: Not specified.
        Gender: Female only.
        Application Deadline: 31 December 2023.
        Official Portal Source: https://www.drdo.gov.in.
        Direct Application Portal: https://rac.gov.in.
        Status: EXPIRED. Scheme closed on 31st December 2023.
