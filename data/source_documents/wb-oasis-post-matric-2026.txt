GOVERNMENT OF WEST BENGAL - OASIS SCHOLARSHIP PORTAL.
        POST-MATRIC SCHOLARSHIP FOR SC/ST/OBC.
        Eligibility Criteria: Students belonging to SC/ST/OBC communities who are permanent residents of West Bengal studying in post-matriculation courses.
        Academic Requirements: Passed previous qualifying exam and studying in Class 11, 12, Graduation or Post-Graduation.
        Financial Benefit Amount: ₹300 to ₹1,200 per month maintenance allowance plus compulsory course fees.
        Family Income Limit: Annual family income not exceeding ₹2.5 Lakh for SC/ST and ₹1.0 Lakh for OBC.
        Category: SC, ST, OBC.
        Domicile: State of West Bengal only.
        Application Deadline: 31 January 2027.
        Official Portal Source: https://oasis.gov.in.
        Direct Application Portal: https://oasis.gov.in.
        Selection Process: Verification by institution head, Block Development Officer (BDO) / Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO).
        Documents Required: West Bengal digital caste certificate, Income certificate, Bank passbook copy with IFSC, Marksheet of last examination.
