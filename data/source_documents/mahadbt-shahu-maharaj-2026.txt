GOVERNMENT OF MAHARASHTRA - MAHADBT PORTAL.
        RAJARSHI CHHATRAPATI SHAHU MAHARAJ SHIKSHAN SHULKH SHISHYAVRUTTI YOJNA.
        Eligibility Criteria: Domicile of Maharashtra admitted to technical / professional undergraduate degree courses through Centralized Admission Process (CAP). Maximum 2 children per family.
        Academic Requirements: Admitted through CAP round in recognized institution in Maharashtra.
        Financial Benefit Amount: 50% Tuition Fee and Exam Fee reimbursement.
        Family Income Limit: Annual family income up to ₹8.00 Lakh from all sources.
        Domicile: State of Maharashtra only.
        Application Deadline: 31 December 2026.
        Official Portal Source: https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in.
        Direct Application Portal: https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in.
        Selection Process: Direct benefit transfer (DBT) verification by college nodal officer and department sanction.
        Documents Required: Domicile certificate of Maharashtra, Income certificate from Tahsildar, CAP allotment letter, Fee receipt, Attendance certificate.
