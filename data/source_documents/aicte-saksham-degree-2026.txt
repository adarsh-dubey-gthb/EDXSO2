AICTE STUDENT DEVELOPMENT SCHEMES - SAKSHAM SCHOLARSHIP.
        Implemented by All India Council for Technical Education.
        Eligibility Criteria: Specially-abled student having disability of not less than 40% admitted to 1st year of Degree level course in AICTE approved institution.
        Academic Requirements: Admission to 1st year or 2nd year lateral entry in AICTE approved college.
        Financial Benefit: ₹50,000 per annum for every year of study towards fees and equipment.
        Family Income Limit: Family annual income not exceeding ₹8 Lakh per annum.
        Category: PwD (Persons with Disabilities).
        Application Deadline: 31 October 2026.
        Official Application Portal: https://scholarships.gov.in.
        Documents Required: Disability certificate issued by competent medical authority (minimum 40%), Income certificate, Admission letter, Fee receipt, Aadhaar card.
        Selection Process: All eligible specially-abled candidates who apply through NSP and fulfill criteria are awarded.
