L'OREAL INDIA - FOR YOUNG WOMEN IN SCIENCE SCHOLARSHIP (2023 CYCLE).
        Eligibility Criteria: Female students who passed Class 12 in Science stream with minimum 85% in PCM/PCB during academic year 2022-23.
