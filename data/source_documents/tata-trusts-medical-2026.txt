TATA TRUSTS - MEDICAL AND HEALTHCARE HIGHER EDUCATION GRANTS.
        Eligibility Criteria: Students pursuing Bachelor's or Master's degrees in Medical, Dental, and Allied Health Sciences in recognized medical colleges in India.
        Academic Requirements: NEET qualified with minimum 60% in 1st/subsequent year professional university examinations.
        Course Level: Undergraduate / Postgraduate (MBBS, BDS, MD, MS).
        Financial Benefit: Partial tuition fee grant covering 30% to 70% of fees.
        Family Income Limit: Family annual income not more than ₹6 Lakh per annum.
        Application Deadline: 31 January 2027.
        Official Portal Source: https://www.tatatrusts.org/our-work/individual-grants-programme/education-grants.
        Direct Application Portal: https://www.tatatrusts.org/our-work/individual-grants-programme/education-grants.
        Selection Process: Internal screening by healthcare grants committee.
        Documents Required: NEET scorecard, College admission proof, Detailed fee receipt, Parent income proof, Identity card.
